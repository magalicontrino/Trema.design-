Forty-eight posts, sixteen rows: one photograph, one line, one photograph. The feed is built on the message house and reads from the top — the roof, then the three pillars: everything about your place always up to date, found everywhere, and followers turning into regulars.

Three rows in, someone should know that a trema is the online address of a place, that it is already built from what they publish on Instagram and Google Maps, and that it stays current on its own. Captions below, in the order they go out.
