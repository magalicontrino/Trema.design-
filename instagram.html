Communication

Everything about
your place. One link.

Thirty-three posts, eleven rows: one composition, one photograph, one composition. The feed is built on the message house and reads from the top — the roof, then the three pillars: everything about your place always up to date, found everywhere, and followers turning into regulars.

Three rows in, someone should know that a trema is the online address of a place, that it is already built from what they publish on Instagram and Google Maps, and that it stays current on its own. Captions below, in the order they go out.

Aucune mise en page n'est inventee ici : chaque cadre de texte est une composition nommee, avec ses mots a elle. Une photo entre chaque, et les compositions qui portent deja une image comptent comme des photos. Les fonds alternent un noir un blanc, les photos un lieu un objet. Survole un cadre pour lire le nom de sa composition.
