Communication

Twelve frames,
in order

A second grid, photographs only, laid out in the order they would go out. It is not a feed yet: it is the shortlist, shown at the size and the ratio of the real profile so that what gets judged here is what would actually be seen.

Each frame is numbered — a swap can be asked for by number. Once the twelve and their order are settled, they get merged into the main feed one photograph, one text, one photograph, so that every picture is answered by a line.

Photo, text, photo

What the merge would look like, in miniature. The photographs carry the place, the texts carry the message, and neither has to do the other's job. Read down the column rather than across: on a profile grid, alternating one for one puts a text next to a photograph on every row, which is what keeps the block from reading as a mood board.

Twelve photographs and twelve texts make twenty-four — eight complete rows, the length the feed is already at.
