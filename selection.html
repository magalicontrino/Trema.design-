Every photograph,
numbered

Everything made for the account, in one place. The numbers are fixed: number 3 stays number 3 whatever gets added underneath, so a choice can be made out loud — we like 3, 2 and 5 — without describing anything.

Shown cropped to 4:5, the ratio of the profile, so what is picked is what would be seen. Anything new lands at the bottom with the next number.

Photo, text, photo

Once the picks are settled, this is how they merge into the feed: one photograph, one line, one photograph. The pictures carry the place, the texts carry the message, and neither has to do the other's job. Twelve of each make twenty-four — eight complete rows, the length the feed is already at.

Shown with the first six picks. Change the picks and this changes with them.
